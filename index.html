RunScore

RunScore is a composite index I built that scores all 3,143 U.S. counties on the strength of their running culture, using CDC health data, race participation records, walkability scores, and fitness facility density. A 10-point increase in RunScore tracks with about $58,000 in higher median home value, and it also correlates with health outcomes and school quality. I wrote up the methodology in a paper.

RecovHR

RecovHR is a heart-rate-reserve algorithm I built for individualized interval recovery, converting max heart rate, resting heart rate, lactate threshold, and workout type into the one number that tells you when you're recovered enough to go hard again. I wrote up the method in a paper and built a live threshold calculator.
